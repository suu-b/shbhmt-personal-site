As I watch the monsoons go, I stand with gates wide-opened to welcome Autumn. I read a few days ago:  
<br />
_May the fall keep me warm_
<br/>

And yes, this is a prayer for _Dear October_.

---

Nevertheless,  
September was a great month. I spent this past month trying to alleviate many of my shortcomings - both in an emotional and intellectual sense. I am going to talk about them a lot throughout this entry. Moreover, I assumed many technical endeavors on my shoulders. Unfortunately, none of them has seen completion till now. I postponed many of them because I started working on something more important.  
<br/>
I have decided to (re)learn mathematics, deep learning, and probably CNNs also. I intend to develop a nice research-grade project. As of now, I am only able to cover major sections of probability and linear algebra; there is still a long way to go. However, I must admit that looking again at these concepts has cleared my mind a lot. I can be thankful for this partly to my mature/grown mind, which has become well-versed in Computer Science, Computation, and the so-called 'Real-world'. However, I must credit more the personal-pedagogy which I have developed in the last few months. 'Intuition, ' I have concluded, is the most important piece of learning anything. While enumerating the concepts of Probability and Linear Algebra, I have always tried to keep up this 'intuition-seeking' spirit. Aristotelian Syllogism and building Mental Models have been really helpful in this 'intuition-led' pedagogical style. Accompanied by Socratic questioning, this overall approach ensures I am assumption-aware and really on the 'correct' path. Also, it enables me to look at things in two ways - fundamentally and broadly; both are extremely revealing. Looking at things fundamentally reveals the 'why' in an existential sense. Looking at things broadly gives us a bird's-eye view of how the system works. This is another kind of 'why' we might be interested in.
<br/>

Moreover, I have decided that I shall not do any math on paper. Instead, I will use Python and its built-in libraries. The reason is twofold: 1. I do not have much time, 2. This will strip the labor of actually doing the calculations and the tedious stuff. It will enable me to do higher-order thinking so that I can run trials and experiment with approaches. Have not yet started, but have planned to.  
Also, an idea struck me - to build a personalized YouTube. It sounds too plain at first sight. It is not. It will not be just a regular recommender. Rather, I seek to integrate it with this site so that it can get a broader context. Moreover, I don't want videos all over the place rather neatly arranged as per my current endeavors - technical or non-technical, with a reasoning why they were suggested. There are many other things too, but I feel this is not the best place to talk about them.
<br/>

A thought struck my mind just now - learning computer science has really empowered me. I can make my own site, an article CRM, and even a custom YouTube! All tailored for my taste.
<br/>

On my artistic side, I can see the 'Poet' cooling down. I am not writing any poems nowadays. Instead, my mind is waiting for the results of the submission I have already made.

---

September gave me so much. I am happier nowadays. I am a bit more grown-up. Haha. I am doing things systematically. I am learning. And relearning. I look forward to October. I want to see where it will take me in both professional and personal terms.  
Dear October, please keep me warm.

~ suu-b
