The answer to this question is twofold: first, it deals with the general reason for one to make a personal site, and second, it deals with the personal motivation (or need).

### General Reasons

What does an artist need the most, except passion and freedom? Building a personal site is no less than [doing art](https://suub.netlify.app/article/meditation/2025-09-04/on-art-and-for-the-artist). Even a drop of passion can lead to infinite possibilities! Moreover, once you realize that you bear immense creative freedom. You are not condemned to be free here, [at least here](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jean-Paul_Sartre#Thought) haha. You can control everything. In addition, in this [Vibe Coding](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vibe_coding) era, you can control every tiny bit of your site - from design to actual deployment. That is fascinating, isn't it?  
Btw, there are many other good reasons to create one. [Here](https://logangraves.com/website) is a wonderful guide and article on all the whys and hows.

### Personal Reasons

Here comes a bit of history.  
Well, last year, I started a [Medium account](https://medium.com/@suu-b) to write some articles. Partly, as an outlet for my teeming brain, and partly to learn something from the actual community. However, the motivation gradually flattened. The reasons were - busy schedule, inconsistency, and a difference in taste with the platform.  
I jumped to [Substack](https://suub.substack.com/). I love the Substack community from the bottom of my heart. I did not feel any difference in taste while there. I wrote a few articles and notes. However, later again, I stopped publishing anything.  
As of now, I avoid visiting Medium very often; however, in my free time, I often spend my time on Substack.  
I started writing on LinkedIn, too. However, could not keep it consistent.

Maintaining a personal site is an initiative to solve the problem of not being consistent. I want to overcome this evil of mine. Moreover, replacing every platform with a personal site really cleans the surface. Now, there is just one place for me to write and send my writings to the world. This is just like writing a book.  
Another merit (which to someone is not a merit) is that I have not yet set up some way to track traffic flow on this site. Why? I don't know how to do so. Maybe the hosting platform can do so (with a bit of money), but I don't want it, actually. Why?
<br/>
_Screaming into an empty cave and not hearing an echo back is a way better option than trying to talk with a mute crowd. This is a 'personal site vs other platforms' in a nutshell. I prefer to talk with the void. If, somehow, any ear swept in and heard my musings, I will feel blessed._
<br/>
And to the reader, I say, this site is a mere preface to me since no human can condense their depth into finite pages. These articles serve as a mere bridge between you and me; there is more to me, as much as there is more to you.
