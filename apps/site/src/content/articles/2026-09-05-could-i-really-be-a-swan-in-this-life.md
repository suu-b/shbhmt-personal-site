This morning, I woke up relatively early - maybe a couple of hours - earlier than my usual wake-up time. For some reason, I woke up with a sheer urge to watch this Nutcracker ballet performance - [From Mariinsky Theatre](https://youtu.be/xtLoaMfinbU). Actually, I have been growing an interest in Western classical music for the last two or three years. I love Tchaikovsky so much. I ended up discovering him and his pieces (particularly The Nutcracker) via [Tom and Jerry's Movie](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Tom_and_Jerry:_A_Nutcracker_Tale). Note that Christmas is not a major festival in our culture, so this was the only way for me to find out about this whimsical ballet.  But what exactly grew this ballet on me was [Vladimir Shklyarov](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vladimir_Shklyarov) and [Alina Somova](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Alina_Somova)'s performance of The Nutcracker: Scene VIII - Scene In The Pine Forest.  

***

Nevertheless, the point here is - I often feel fearful that I wouldn't be able to do everything I want in this life. I have a lot of dreams - studying and doing some real work at some big university - studying art, literature, music, computer science - creating impact in these fields - creating something of personal and societal meaning - owning and maintaining a farm, a garden... god knows what more. But sometimes I doubt if this all is really possible. I turned 21 last month. My twenties have officially started, and I am still here wherever I am. I am well aware that life can take very sharp, dramatic, drastic turns even in a day. But still, I feel I am really, really off track. But again, this isn't a race or even a marathon haha. 
<br/>
The only advice I have for myself is to keep faith in myself, the things I do, and the aspirations I have. God will take care of the rest.  

Addendum:
I used Swan as a symbol of the freed, graceful, artistic, intelligent (kinda all figured out) version of myself. Let's cut the 'all figured out' part, but yes, a future version of me who is something I wish to be like.
